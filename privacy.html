Privacy Policy — Ricoh-Drive-Backup

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Ricoh-Drive-Backup is a personal-use tool. This policy describes what it does with your data.

What the app does

The app backs up JPG photos from a Ricoh GR camera SD card to the owner's own Google Drive folder. It lists the files already in that Drive folder to avoid uploading duplicates, and uploads only new photos.

Data collected and used

  • Photos on the connected SD card (read only, to upload them to the owner's Drive).
  • File names and file sizes of photos already in the owner's Google Drive folder (used only to check for duplicates).
  • Google account credentials, stored only on the owner's iPhone in the iOS Keychain, used only to sign in to the owner's own Google Drive.

Data sharing

The app does not share, sell, or disclose any personal data to any third party. Photos and credentials are sent only between the owner's iPhone, the owner's SD card, and the owner's Google Drive account.

Data retention

The app keeps a small local list of uploaded file names and sizes on the owner's iPhone, only to avoid duplicate uploads. It does not keep copies of photos on any server of its own, because it has no server.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is used only by its owner.
